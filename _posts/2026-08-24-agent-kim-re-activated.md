title: "I Didn't Plan to Watch a Korean Drama. Then Came Agent Kim."

Watching a Korean drama was definitely not on my 2026 list, but *Agent Kim Re-Activated*, a Netflix series, changed that for me.

One weekend, while I was aimlessly scrolling through Netflix, I came across a title called *Agent Kim Re-Activated*. The word "agent" caught my eye, so I read the synopsis. It was compelling enough that I decided to give it a shot. It's one of those series that falls neatly into the kind of movies and shows I generally enjoy. And I'm glad I watched it. I don't regret it one bit.

*Agent Kim Re-Activated* is the story of a man living an ordinary life as a bank employee while hiding a secret past. He is also a single father. When something terrible happens and his beloved daughter goes missing, Agent Kim embarks on a dangerous journey to find her, along with his old friends.

I don't want to reveal too much, but this series has got everything: the emotions of a father-daughter relationship, coming-of-age themes, school bullying, friendships, action, sleek action by the way, and a sprinkle of comedy every now and then.

The protagonist, played by Korean actor So Ji-sub, does a convincing job as a former spy-turned-single father. He is ably supported by the other two dads, Park Jin Cheol and Seong Han Su, who also happen to be his old friends and former colleagues from his spy days.

Agent Kim is a man of few words. He is reticent and doesn't have a lot of dialogue throughout the series, but he does a great job with the few words he does get. He gives off *John Wick* vibes at times, although, well, John Wick is John Wick. Agent Kim's daughter also leaves a mark with her performance. There is something very believable about the relationship between the two, which makes the emotional parts of the story work.

There were quite a few high points for me in this series, and they made it thoroughly enjoyable. The one that tops the list involves Park Jin Cheol. In one scene, the villain's muscleman, his head of security, gives a pretty brutal beating to the police officers who show up at the villain's mansion. And then, in the very next episode, Park Jin Cheol, Agent Kim's friend and easily the funniest guy in the series, gives it right back to him. The muscleman is visibly terrified. Just a little while ago, he had taken down an entire bunch of police officers, and now here is this guy, a dad in his late forties, beating the daylights out of him. I probably watched that scene multiple times. You can't help but root for Park Jin Cheol there.

And then there is another scene where Agent Kim confronts the villain. The villain goes on a long monologue about everything he has to offer and how they should bury the hatchet. He then tries to buy out Agent Kim, asks him to join his side and take the money he is offering. Agent Kim just looks at him. And finally, he says that he wasn't sure what he was going to do with the villain when they met.

Then he says:

> "Now I know what I'm gonna do. I'm going to end you."

That was such a cold moment. And then he proceeds to badly beat the villain. There was nothing particularly groundbreaking about that scene, but the way Agent Kim delivers that line was fantastic. It was one of those moments that is just thoroughly enjoyable.

Then there is the friendly banter between Park Jin Cheol and Seong Han Su. I particularly enjoyed how Park Jin Cheol tries to act like a sigma outside, but when he is with his wife, he is almost always the subdued one. There are also these two agents who are sort of shadowing Agent Kim while working undercover. They have their own fun moments, too. All of these little things are what make *Agent Kim Re-Activated* work.

And then, of course, there are the cliches - Agent Kim is a man of honour. He keeps his word. He puts his life on the line for his friends' kids. He shows up when everything seems lost and saves the day at the very end. It's all there. But honestly, no matter how many times I see these cliche moments across different series and movies, I still enjoy them. There is something comforting about watching a character who has a strong sense of honour, who refuses to abandon his people and who will go to any lengths to protect them.

*Agent Kim Re-Activated* may not be everyone's cup of tea. But if you like spy thrillers and action with a dash of comedy, mixed with a father-daughter relationship and a father trying to do everything and anything in the world to keep his daughter safe, you'll probably enjoy this one.

Go check it out on Netflix.
